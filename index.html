Pad Sites Available

San Sebastian is a 47-acre modern community combining residential living with commercial convenience. Eight subdivided and engineered pad sites front Business 77, offering exceptional visibility and access. Sizes range from 1.01 AC to 1.60 AC with full infrastructure — drainage, water, and sewer easements in place.

Strategic Location — San Benito, TX (Cameron County), one of the fastest-growing markets in the Rio Grande Valley. 24,000+ residents with continued growth projections. Direct access to Oscar Williams Rd and minutes from I-69E.
