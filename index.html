Questions & Answers

What Buyers
Ask Us Most

Straight answers on lots, pricing, utilities, taxes, flood zone and schools. If your question is not here, ask us directly.

Where exactly is San Sebastian?

The community sits on 47.35 acres fronting W Business 77 on the north side of San Benito, in Cameron County, just northwest of the Oscar Williams Road (FM 1846) intersection and roughly midway between Harlingen and Brownsville.

All eight commercial pad sites front Business 77 directly. The 185 residential lots sit behind them on internal streets. Interstate 69E is about four minutes away.

What is available right now, and what do lots cost?

As of September 2026, 51 residential lots are available, priced from $64,179 to $140,299. A further 54 Phase 3 lots are offered as pre-sale. All eight commercial pad sites are available; pricing for those is on request.

Availability and pricing change as lots sell. The interactive lot map always shows the current position.

How big are the lots, and what can I build?

Residential lots range from 4,711 to 15,167 square feet. Lots available today run from 6,938 to 15,167 square feet.

Every lot accommodates a minimum home footprint of 1,300 square feet, with one- or two-story designs and a two-car garage.

Commercial pad sites range from 1.01 to 1.60 acres, subdivided and engineered, with drainage, water and sewer easements in place.

How are the phases laid out?

Phase 1 is complete and selling, with 56 finished lots and full infrastructure on the western edge.

Phase 2 is selling, adding 72 lots with the primary roadway extended toward North Oscar Williams Road.

Phase 3 adds the final 57 lots and extends the road to West Business 77, completing the 185-lot plan.

Is the property in a flood zone?

Per FEMA's National Flood Hazard Layer, the site is mapped in Flood Zone X, an area of minimal flood hazard, outside the Special Flood Hazard Area. This is FIRM panel 48061C0270F, effective 16 February 2018.

Zone X means flood insurance is not federally mandated. It does not mean there is no flood risk. Buyers should obtain an independent elevation certificate and confirm requirements with their lender and insurer.

Which school district serves the community?

The community lies within San Benito Consolidated Independent School District, confirmed by coordinate against U.S. Census school district boundaries.

Attendance zones are set by the district and can change, so confirm specific campus assignments directly with San Benito CISD before you rely on them.

Who provides utilities?

Water and wastewater are provided by the City of San Benito Utilities Department.

Electricity is delivered by AEP Texas, which operates the poles and wires. Texas is a deregulated market, so you choose your own retail electricity provider.

For natural gas availability at a specific lot, ask us and we will confirm with the developer.

What are the property taxes?

Using 2025 adopted rates published by the Cameron Appraisal District, the combined rate for a property in San Benito is approximately $2.37 per $100 of assessed valuation. That figure combines:

  • San Benito CISD — 1.050600
  • City of San Benito — 0.708125
  • Cameron County — 0.424393
  • Drainage District No. 3 — 0.137671
  • South Texas ISD — 0.049200

Each taxing unit sets its own rate annually and rates change. Confirm the current rate and the units that apply to a specific parcel with the Cameron County Tax Office.

How is the property zoned?

San Sebastian is zoned as a Planned Development District, adopted by the City of San Benito on 5 December 2024. The master plan was approved unanimously on 21 January 2025, and a PDD amendment, Ordinance 2252-W-A-010626, passed its second and final reading on 6 January 2026.

The PDD framework is what allows residential and commercial uses to sit together on one tract.

What is the traffic count on Business 77?

TxDOT's 2025 annual average daily traffic for the Business 77 segment the property fronts is 21,883 vehicles per day. The two count stations bracketing the frontage report 20,115 and 21,883.

For comparison, TxDOT records roughly 6,100 vehicles per day on FM 1846 (Oscar Williams Road) and about 94,300 on the I-69E mainlanes nearby.

Is there an HOA, and what are the dues?

Yes. Membership in the San Sebastian Homeowners Association is mandatory for every lot owner, and annual dues are $360. The recorded Covenants, Conditions and Restrictions govern the community and supersede any earlier restrictions.

All building plans and any later changes to a home, yard or driveway need approval from the San Sebastian Architectural Committee first. Common rules cover trash can screening, yard upkeep, parking in the garage or driveway only, no boats, trailers or RVs, and a prohibition on aggressive dog breeds. Owners who rent take responsibility for their tenants.

Read the full documents in the Documents section.
