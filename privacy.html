1. Information we collect

Information you give us

The only personal information this site collects is what you choose to enter in one of the two contact forms (Commercial Pad Sites or Residential Lots):

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Your area of interest (residential form only, such as a specific phase)
  • Your message

Along with your entries, the form records which form you used and the web address of the page you submitted it from, so we know which listing your inquiry relates to.

Information collected automatically

Like virtually every website, the hosting provider that serves these pages receives standard technical information from your browser when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested. That information is used for delivering the site and for security, not for building a profile of you.

The site also uses Google Analytics to measure how visitors use it, in aggregate. It records which sections are viewed, how far down the page people scroll, and which buttons are used, such as a call, an email, a brochure download, or a form submission. It records these as counts and categories, not as a record of you personally, and we do not use it for advertising. Advertising features and ad personalisation are switched off, and your IP address is anonymised. Analytics does not run at all if your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, and it does not run unless measurement has been switched on by the site operator. See Section 4 for details.

2. How we use it

We use the information you submit to:

  • Respond to your inquiry about commercial pad sites or residential lots at San Sebastian
  • Answer your questions, schedule site tours, and send you information you have requested about the community
  • Keep a record of inquiries received about the property

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for automated marketing lists, and we do not share it with unrelated third parties for their own marketing.

5. Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies of its own. Google Analytics sets cookies controlled by Google to tell a returning visit from a new one; these are used for measurement only, never for advertising. You can block cookies in your browser settings without affecting how the site works, and analytics is skipped entirely if your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

The site no longer embeds an interactive map. The location section uses a static aerial image, so no map provider receives your data from this page. The "Get Directions" button is an ordinary link; Google receives your information only after you choose to follow it.

6. How long we keep information

Inquiries are kept in the recipients' email and brokerage records for as long as needed to respond to you and to maintain a record of interest in the property, which typically means for the duration of the community's sales program and any period afterward required by Texas real estate record-keeping rules. You may ask us to delete your inquiry at any time; see the next section.

7. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and form submissions are transmitted over an encrypted connection. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You do not have to submit a form. You can call or email the brokers directly using the contact information at the bottom of the main page.
  • Access, correction, or deletion. You may ask us what information we hold about your inquiry, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details in Section 11. We will honor reasonable requests unless we are required to keep the information by law.
  • Opting out of follow-up. If you no longer want to hear from us about San Sebastian, reply to any message and say so, or contact us directly.
  • Do Not Track. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, this site does not load analytics at all. No measurement script runs and no analytics cookie is set.
  • Opting out of measurement. You can also block analytics with any standard content blocker, or with Google's own opt-out browser add-on. Blocking it does not affect how the site works.

Residents of states with consumer privacy laws that grant additional rights may exercise those rights through the same contact channels. This site is intended for visitors in the United States.

9. Children

This site markets real estate to adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through this site, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site or the services it uses change. The effective date at the top of the page will be updated when we do. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact us

For any question about this policy or the information you have submitted, contact the site operator. For residential-inquiry records, you may also contact Alliance Real Estate Group directly.
